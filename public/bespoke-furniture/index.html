Bespoke and custom furniture made to your drawings, in Dubai

Calibro makes bespoke and custom furniture for architects, interior designers and their clients in Dubai and the UAE: sofas and seating, dining and coffee tables, beds and headboards, consoles and casegoods. Each piece is detailed in a shop drawing, sampled in your specified finishes and, for critical items, prototyped, then produced in an audited UAE partner workshop and installed by Calibro.

Working with your drawings

The drawing is the contract. Calibro reviews what you send, details every piece in a shop drawing and returns it for your approval. Finishes are sampled, critical pieces are prototyped, and nothing changes without your signature.

What to send

  • Plans, elevations and sections of each piece, or a dimensioned reference image
  • A finishes schedule or specification: timber, veneer, stone, metal, fabric and leather references
  • Quantities, the programme and the site address
  • A BOQ, if one exists; itemised pricing follows your schedule

What comes back

  • Questions, raised before they become problems on site
  • Shop drawings for every piece, for approval before production
  • Physical samples of each finish, and a prototype of each critical piece
  • A programme with the hold points where you sign off

Questions about bespoke furniture

Do you make single pieces or whole packages?

Both. Calibro makes a single feature piece to a designer's drawing and complete furniture packages for a residence, a reception or a workplace, coordinated under one programme.

What do you need from a designer to quote?

Drawings of each piece (plans, elevations and sections, or a clear reference image with dimensions), a finishes schedule or specification, quantities and the programme. A BOQ helps but is not required. Calibro replies with questions, then an itemised quotation.

Can you match a specified fabric, leather or veneer?

Yes. Specified materials are sourced as named, or a matched alternative is proposed with a physical sample for the designer's written approval. Nothing is substituted without that approval.

Do you prototype before production?

For critical pieces, yes. A prototype or first-off is reviewed against the shop drawing and the approved samples before the rest of the order is made.

Where is the furniture made?

In audited partner workshops in the UAE, chosen for the materials and scale of each project. Calibro does not own a factory; it owns the shop drawings, the supervision, the hold-point inspections and the release decision.
