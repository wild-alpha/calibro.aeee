Made in audited UAE partner workshops, to Calibro's drawings

Calibro does not own a factory. Production is placed with audited partner workshops in the UAE, selected for each project's materials, scale and programme. Calibro owns the shop drawings, the supervision, the hold-point inspections and the release decision, and remains the single point of responsibility from the first drawing to aftercare.

Why partner workshops

No single workshop is the best at everything. A penthouse package needs upholstery, veneered casegoods, stone, metalwork and lacquer finishing at the same standard. Calibro places each scope with the workshop best equipped for it, and keeps the engineering, the supervision, the inspection and the responsibility in one place.

It also means Calibro's capacity follows the project rather than the other way round: a single feature piece and a hundred-piece package are produced in the right place for each, without a factory floor that has to be kept busy.

How a workshop is audited

Partners are not named publicly.
Capability
Machinery, capacity and the skills for the scope: veneer pressing, CNC and hand joinery, upholstery, metal fabrication, stone, spray finishing.
Materials
Sourcing, storage and conditioning of timber, veneers, foams, fabrics and finishes, with traceability to the approved samples.
Quality and records
Documented procedures, inspection records and corrective actions, and the willingness to work to Calibro's hold points.
Reliability and safety
Programme performance, and health and safety on the floor.

Quality control at six hold points

Production stops at each hold point until the inspection is passed.
  1. 01

    Shop drawings

    Approved by the designer before anything is cut.

  2. 02

    Samples

    Finishes approved against the specified references.

  3. 03

    First-off

    A prototype or first piece reviewed against the drawing before the order runs.

  4. 04

    In production

    Construction and finish checked at the workshop by Calibro.

  5. 05

    Pre-delivery release

    Each piece scored out of 100; released at 90 or above, otherwise corrected and re-inspected.

  6. 06

    Installation

    Checked in place, snag list closed at handover.

Questions about production

Why doesn't Calibro own a factory?

Because no single workshop is the best at everything. A penthouse package needs upholstery, veneered casegoods, stone, metalwork and lacquer finishing at the same standard; Calibro places each scope with the audited partner workshop best equipped for it, and keeps the engineering, supervision, inspection and responsibility in one place.

How is a partner workshop audited?

Against Calibro's criteria for machinery and capacity, materials handling and storage, finishing facilities, quality procedures and records, health and safety, and programme reliability.

Who inspects the work?

Calibro, at six hold points: approved shop drawings, approved samples, the first-off or prototype, in-production checks, the pre-delivery release and the installation snag list. Each piece is scored out of 100 at release and leaves the workshop only at 90 or above.

Can a client visit the workshop?

Yes, by arrangement, for prototype reviews and in-production inspections. Calibro accompanies every visit.
