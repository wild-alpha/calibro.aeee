( 06 )The process

Six steps, six sign-offs

Nothing is made, and nothing is substituted, without the designer's written approval.

  1. 01

    Drawings review

    We study your drawings, specification and BOQ, and raise questions before they become problems.

  2. 02

    Shop drawings

    Every piece is detailed for production and returned to you for approval.

  3. 03

    Samples and prototypes

    Finishes, fabrics and prototypes of critical pieces are signed off before production starts.

  4. 04

    Production

    Made in audited UAE partner workshops, under Calibro's supervision.

  5. 05

    Quality control

    Hold-point inspections against the approved drawings before anything leaves the workshop.

  6. 06

    Installation

    Delivered, installed and handed over, with aftercare once the space is in use.

Questions specifiers ask

Does Calibro have its own factory?

No. Calibro produces through audited partner workshops in the UAE, chosen for each project's materials and scale. Calibro owns the engineering, the shop drawings, quality control, delivery and installation, and stays your single point of responsibility.

Do you work from an interior designer's drawings?

Yes. Most projects start from an architect's or interior designer's drawings and specification. Calibro details every piece in shop drawings and returns them for approval before anything is made.

What kinds of projects does Calibro deliver?

Private residences, penthouses and villas, building receptions and workplaces in Dubai, including Il Primo, Sobha – The S Tower, Grand Bleu Tower 1, Dubai Hills Fairways and a 2,185 m² office at Vision Tower.

Can Calibro value-engineer a furniture specification?

Yes. Calibro proposes material and construction alternatives that bring a package into budget or programme while protecting the design intent. Every change is submitted for the designer's written approval.

What should I send to get a quotation?

Drawings, a specification or finishes schedule, and a BOQ if you have one. An early sketch is enough to start. Calibro replies with questions, an approach and a timeline.

Where is Calibro based?

Calibro's studio is on 47 Street, Jebel Ali Industrial Area, Dubai, by appointment. The company is Calibro Furniture Trading FZE, licensed in Ajman Free Zone (licence 53390).
