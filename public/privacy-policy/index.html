Privacy policy

This policy explains how Calibro Furniture Trading FZE ("Calibro") handles the personal data you send through calibro.ae and Calibro Workspace. It is written to comply with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data.

Who is responsible

Calibro Furniture Trading FZE, licensed in Ajman Free Zone (licence 53390), with a studio at 47 Street, Jebel Ali Industrial Area, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. For anything about your data, write to info@calibro.ae.

What we collect

  • Enquiries: the name, company, email, phone number, project details and files you send through the contact form, WhatsApp or email.
  • Calibro Workspace: the account details you register, the documents you upload and the requests and bookings you make.
  • Website use: this website runs no analytics and no advertising trackers, so nothing about your visit is collected beyond the standard technical logs the hosting service keeps for security.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, prepare quotations and run your project; to operate your Workspace account; to meet legal and accounting duties. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

Who sees it

The Calibro team, and the service providers that host the website (Vercel), the Workspace (Netlify) and our email (Google Workspace), who act on our instructions. Their servers may be outside the UAE. Drawings and specifications are shared with the partner workshop producing a piece only as far as the project requires, under confidentiality.

How long we keep it

Enquiry data is kept while the enquiry and any project arising from it are active, and afterwards for as long as UAE commercial and tax law requires us to keep contractual records. Workspace accounts are kept until you ask for them to be closed.

Your rights

You may ask to see the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted where we no longer need it, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw a consent you have given. Write to info@calibro.ae; we reply within one month.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies. Calibro Workspace, a separate site, uses the sign-in storage it needs to keep you signed in.

Changes

This policy was last updated on 9 November 2026. Changes are published on this page with the new date.
