The studio

Calibro is a Dubai studio for bespoke and custom furniture, joinery and contract FF&E. It takes the drawings of architects and interior designers and delivers them as built pieces: detailed in shop drawings, sampled and approved, produced in audited UAE partner workshops, installed and looked after. Calibro Furniture Trading FZE was founded in 2025 on 18+ years of team experience, with a studio in Jebel Ali.

What we do

Architects and interior designers draw interiors that cannot be bought from a catalogue. Calibro exists to build those drawings as they were imagined: the sofa at the proportion the room needs, the wardrobe to the millimetre of the wall, the reception desk as the architect detailed it.

Five capabilities sit under one contract: bespoke furniture, joinery, contract FF&E supply and procurement, value engineering, and delivery, installation and aftercare. The projects so far are private residences and penthouses, a workplace and a hotel reception in Dubai, each credited to its designer.

How we work

The drawing is the contract. Every piece is detailed in a shop drawing and returned for approval; finishes are sampled and critical pieces prototyped; production stops at six hold points for inspection; each piece is scored out of 100 and released at 90 or above; installation ends with a closed snag list. Value engineering changes what does not show, never what the client sees, and every change needs the designer's written approval. The six steps, in detail.

Where it is made

Calibro does not own a factory, and says so.

Production is placed with audited partner workshops in the UAE, chosen for each project's materials, scale and programme: joinery, upholstery, metalwork, stone and finishing. Calibro owns the shop drawings, the supervision, the hold-point inspections and the release decision, and remains the single point of responsibility from the first drawing to aftercare.

The name

Calibro is Italian for a caliper, the instrument that measures a piece against its drawing. It is also the word for calibre. Both say what the studio is for: the built piece should match the drawing, exactly. Hence the line under the name, Exactly as drawn.
