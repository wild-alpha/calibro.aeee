Survey, shop drawings, finishes

Fixed joinery is only as good as its survey.
Survey
Walls, floors, ceilings and services are measured as built. Where the site is not ready, the shop drawings carry the tolerances and the survey follows before production.
Shop drawings
Construction, substrates, hardware, lighting and services, every dimension against the survey, returned for the designer's approval before anything is cut.
Finishes
Natural and reconstituted veneers, stains, oils and lacquers, laminates, stone tops, metal trims and integrated lighting, each sampled and approved.
Installation
Fitted and scribed on site by Calibro's team to the contractor's programme, with the snag list closed at handover.

For fit-out contractors

One package for fixed joinery and loose furniture, under one contract and one programme. Shop drawings are submitted for the designer's approval, hold points are agreed with your site team, and the installation is sequenced with your trades.

  • Delivery windows and installation you can programme around

Questions about joinery

Do you survey the site before making joinery?

Yes. Fixed joinery is detailed against a site survey, not the architectural drawing alone, so wardrobes, panelling and vanities meet the walls, floors and services as built. Where the site is not ready, the shop drawings carry the tolerances and the survey follows before production.

Can you work as a joinery subcontractor to a fit-out contractor?

Yes. Calibro supplies and installs joinery and loose furniture as one package under the contractor's programme, with shop drawings submitted for the designer's approval and hold points agreed with the contractor.

Which finishes can be specified?

Natural and reconstituted veneers, stains, oils and lacquers, laminates, stone tops, metal trims and integrated lighting. Each finish is sampled in the specified reference and approved before production.

Where is the joinery made?

In audited partner workshops in the UAE, chosen for the project. Calibro details, supervises, inspects at hold points and installs; it does not own a factory.
