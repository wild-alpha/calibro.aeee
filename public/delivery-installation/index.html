Delivery, installation and aftercare

  1. 01

    Site readiness

    Confirmed before anything leaves the workshop.

    Finished floors, walls and ceilings in the rooms concerned, services in place where joinery connects to them, clear access and a protected route from the loading point. Calibro checks readiness with the site team and advises on sequencing with the other trades.

  2. 02

    Delivery

    Released pieces, protected, delivered in sequence.

    Pieces released at quality control are wrapped and crated for the route, delivered to the programme and brought to the room in the order the installation needs. Procured items in an FF&E package travel with them.

  3. 03

    Installation

    Positioned, fixed, levelled and scribed by Calibro's team.

    Loose furniture is positioned to the layout; fixed joinery is fitted, scribed to the walls and connected to services; hardware and mechanisms are adjusted and tested. The team coordinates with the main contractor or the client's site team throughout.

  4. 04

    Snagging and handover

    The snag list walked with you and closed.

    Every piece is checked in place against the approved drawing. The snag list is walked with the designer or client, closed, and the handover documents issued: finish references and care guidance.

  5. 05

    Aftercare

    One contact, for as long as the space is in use.

    Care guidance for each finish, and a single point of contact for adjustments, repairs and re-upholstery once the interior is occupied. Warranty terms are confirmed for each project in the contract.

Questions about installation

Do you install the furniture yourselves?

Yes. Calibro's installation team delivers, positions, fixes and levels every piece, coordinated with the main contractor or the client's site team, and walks the snag list with you before handover.

What does the site need to be ready for installation?

Finished floors, walls and ceilings in the rooms concerned, services in place where joinery connects to them, clear access for the pieces and a protected route from the loading point. Calibro confirms readiness before delivery and advises on sequencing.

What aftercare does Calibro provide?

Care guidance for each finish at handover, and a single point of contact for adjustments and repairs once the space is in use. Warranty terms are confirmed for each project in the contract.

Do you deliver outside Dubai?

Across the UAE.
