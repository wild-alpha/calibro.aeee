Dubai Hills Fairways, Dubai Hills Estate

A bespoke furniture package for a 1,502 m² villa at Fairways, Dubai Hills Estate, engineered and produced by Calibro to the design intent of Jadex Studio: seating for a double-height living room with a marble feature wall, dining furniture, and the beds, headboards and casegoods of the bedrooms, each piece drawn for the house rather than chosen from a collection.

The brief

Jadex Studio conceived the villa as a fully integrated interior, with every furnishing designed for its room and its proportion. The furniture had to respond to the scale of each space, from the double-height living room to the bedrooms, with one palette of materials throughout.

Calibro's scope

Calibro engineered and produced the package. The design is Jadex Studio's; the shop drawings, samples, production in audited UAE partner workshops, quality control, delivery and installation are Calibro's.

  • Living room: sofas and seating at the scale of the double-height space
  • Dining furniture
  • Bedrooms: beds, headboards, bedside and dressing pieces
  • Process: shop drawings, finish samples, hold-point inspections, installation

As photographed

Every photograph of the project, in the order of a walk through the space. Nothing is cropped.

Materials and making

As on every Calibro project, each piece was detailed in a shop drawing for Jadex Studio's approval, sampled in the specified finishes, produced in audited UAE partner workshops and checked against the approved drawing before delivery and installation in the villa. The photographs show the living room seating under the double-height ceiling and a bedroom with its upholstered bed and headboard.
