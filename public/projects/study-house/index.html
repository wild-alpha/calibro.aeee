Study House, Al Khawaneej

A private cinema room at Study House, a 203.9 m² project at Wadi Al Amardi, Al Khawaneej, furnished with bespoke reclining sofas engineered and produced by Calibro to the design intent of ISTO Architecture: two tiers of reclining seating for long viewing sessions.

Start a project like this
Location
Wadi Al Amardi, Al Khawaneej, Dubai
Interior design
ISTO Architecture
Calibro scope
Engineering and production of the bespoke cinema seating
Area
203.9 m²

The brief

ISTO Architecture drew a cinema room where the seating is the architecture: two stepped tiers of recliners, upholstered to match the room's palette, with the mechanisms and the ergonomics hidden inside a calm, continuous form.

Calibro's scope

Calibro engineered and produced the package. The design is ISTO Architecture's; the shop drawings, samples, production in audited UAE partner workshops, quality control, delivery and installation are Calibro's.

  • Two tiers of bespoke reclining sofas
  • Upholstery to the specified fabrics
  • Process: shop drawings, finish samples, hold-point inspections, installation

As photographed

Every photograph of the project, in the order of a walk through the space. Nothing is cropped.

Materials and making

As on every Calibro project, the seating was detailed in shop drawings for ISTO Architecture's approval, sampled in the specified upholstery, produced in an audited UAE partner workshop and checked against the approved drawings before installation on the tiered floor. The photographs show the two tiers of reclining sofas.
