Grand Bleu Tower 1, Emaar Beachfront

Bespoke furniture and fixed joinery for a 195 m² residence at Grand Bleu Tower 1, Emaar Beachfront, engineered and produced by Calibro to the design intent of WEDO Interiors: the living room sofa and shelving, a marble-clad media wall, the dining furniture, and the vanity and wardrobe joinery of the suites.

The brief

WEDO Interiors designed the apartment as one calm palette: pale upholstery, a marble media wall with integrated shelving, and joinery that runs from the living room into the suites. Loose furniture and fixed joinery had to read as a single hand, so both came from one maker under one programme.

Calibro's scope

Calibro engineered and produced the package. The design is WEDO Interiors'; the shop drawings, samples, production in audited UAE partner workshops, quality control, delivery and installation are Calibro's.

  • Living room: sofa, shelving unit and the marble-clad media wall
  • Dining: table and chairs
  • Suites: curved vanity unit and glazed wardrobe joinery
  • Process: shop drawings, finish samples, hold-point inspections, installation

As photographed

Every photograph of the project, in the order of a walk through the space. Nothing is cropped.

Materials and making

As on every Calibro project, the loose furniture and the fixed joinery were detailed in shop drawings for WEDO Interiors' approval, sampled in the specified finishes, produced in audited UAE partner workshops and checked against the approved drawings before installation. The photographs show the white sofa and shelving, the marble-clad media wall, the dining furniture and the curved vanity and glazed wardrobe joinery.
