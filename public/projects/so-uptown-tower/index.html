SO/ Uptown Tower, Uptown Dubai

A fully bespoke furniture package for the 314.6 m² secondary reception at SO/ Uptown Tower, Uptown Dubai, engineered and produced by Calibro with the project team: reception furniture and seating tailored to the layout, circulation and design intent of the arrival space, and built for the demands of a high-traffic hotel environment.

The brief

The secondary reception was conceived as an extension of the tower's contemporary identity: every piece of furniture contributing to the arrival experience, integrated with the interior architecture, and able to take constant use.

Calibro's scope

Calibro engineered and produced the package. The design is the project team's; the shop drawings, samples, production in audited UAE partner workshops, quality control, delivery and installation are Calibro's.

  • Reception furniture tailored to the layout and circulation of the space
  • Seating and tables for the arrival area
  • Contract-grade construction and finishes for a high-traffic hotel environment
  • Process: shop drawings, finish samples, hold-point inspections, installation

As photographed

Every photograph of the project, in the order of a walk through the space. Nothing is cropped.

Materials and making

As on every Calibro project, each piece was detailed in a shop drawing for the project team's approval, sampled in the specified finishes, produced in audited UAE partner workshops and checked against the approved drawing before installation in the reception.
