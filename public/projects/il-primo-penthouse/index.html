Il Primo Penthouse, Downtown Dubai

A complete bespoke furniture package for a 492 m² penthouse at Il Primo Tower, Downtown Dubai, engineered and produced by Calibro to the design intent of WEDO Interiors: modular sofas and lounge chairs for the living room, the dining table and chairs, and the upholstered beds and headboards of the private suites.

The brief

WEDO Interiors designed the penthouse as one continuous interior: living and dining spaces opening onto the skyline, and private suites finished in the same palette. Every piece of furniture was drawn for its position in the room rather than selected from a collection, so each had to be made.

Calibro's task was to take those drawings and deliver them as built pieces, at the scale of a 492 m² residence and to a single programme: upholstered seating sized to the living room's proportions, tables and casegoods in the specified finishes, and beds and headboards detailed to the suites.

Calibro's scope

Calibro engineered and produced the loose furniture package. The design is WEDO Interiors'; the shop drawings, samples, production in audited UAE partner workshops, quality control, delivery and installation are Calibro's.

  • Living room: modular sofas, lounge chairs with curved backs, coffee and side tables
  • Dining room: dining table and dining chairs
  • Private suites: upholstered beds and headboards, bedside and dressing pieces
  • Process: shop drawings for each piece, finish samples and prototypes approved before production, hold-point inspections before delivery, installation on site

Materials and making

As on every Calibro project, each piece was detailed in a shop drawing for WEDO Interiors' approval, sampled in the specified finishes, produced in an audited UAE partner workshop and checked against the approved drawing before it left for the tower, where Calibro's team installed it. The photographs show the upholstered modular seating and lounge chairs, the dining table and chairs, the marble-topped coffee tables and the upholstered beds and headboards.
