Sobha – The S Tower, Dubai

A complete bespoke furniture collection for a 624 m² penthouse at Sobha – The S Tower, Dubai, engineered and produced by Calibro to the design intent of FF+D Architects: sculptural lounge seating around a bronze-clad fireplace wall, and the furniture of the living, dining and private rooms, each piece made to the scale of the penthouse.

The brief

FF+D Architects designed the penthouse with furniture at architectural scale: sculptural armchairs, deep seating and tables proportioned to double-height rooms. Nothing could be bought at that scale, so the whole collection was drawn and had to be made.

Calibro's scope

Calibro engineered and produced the package. The design is FF+D Architects'; the shop drawings, samples, production in audited UAE partner workshops, quality control, delivery and installation are Calibro's.

  • Lounge: sculptural armchairs and seating around the fireplace wall
  • Living and dining furniture to the designer's drawings
  • Private rooms: beds, headboards and casegoods
  • Process: shop drawings, finish samples, hold-point inspections, installation

As photographed

Every photograph of the project, in the order of a walk through the space. Nothing is cropped.

Materials and making

As on every Calibro project, each piece was detailed in a shop drawing for FF+D Architects' approval, sampled in the specified finishes, produced in audited UAE partner workshops and checked against the approved drawing before delivery to the tower.
