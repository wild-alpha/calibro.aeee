Beauport Tower, Dubai Marina

A collection of bespoke furniture for a 202.4 m² residence at Beauport Tower, Dubai Marina, engineered and produced by Calibro to the design intent of M-Studio: a palette of veneers, natural stone, upholstery and metal details carried through the living spaces and the private rooms.

Beauport Tower, Dubai Marina
Interior design

Calibro scope
Engineering and production of the bespoke furniture
Area
202.4 m²

The brief

M-Studio drew a residence in a tight palette of veneers, natural stone, upholstery and metal details, repeated across the living spaces and the suites so that the apartment reads as one piece.

Calibro's scope

Calibro engineered and produced the package. The design is M-Studio's; the shop drawings, samples, production in audited UAE partner workshops, quality control, delivery and installation are Calibro's.

  • Living room seating and tables
  • Dining furniture
  • Bedroom furniture
  • Process: shop drawings, finish samples, hold-point inspections, installation

As photographed

Every photograph of the project, in the order of a walk through the space. Nothing is cropped.

Materials and making

As on every Calibro project, each piece was detailed in a shop drawing for M-Studio's approval, sampled in the specified finishes, produced in audited UAE partner workshops and checked against the approved drawing before installation in the apartment.
