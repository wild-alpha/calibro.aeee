Private Office, Vision Tower, Business Bay

A bespoke furniture package for a 2,185 m² private office at Vision Tower, Business Bay, engineered and produced by Calibro to the design intent of BH Architects: executive workstations, meeting tables, lounge seating and integrated storage, unified in one material palette and built for daily use.

Location
Vision Tower, Business Bay, Dubai
Interior design
BH Architects
Calibro scope
Engineering and production of the bespoke office furniture package
Area
2,185 m²

The brief

BH Architects designed a workplace with the finish of a residence: executive rooms, meeting spaces, collaborative areas and lounges in one palette. The furniture had to carry that finish and stand up to everyday office use.

Calibro's scope

Calibro engineered and produced the package. The design is BH Architects'; the shop drawings, samples, production in audited UAE partner workshops, quality control, delivery and installation are Calibro's.

  • Executive workstations and desking
  • Meeting tables and conference furniture
  • Lounge and collaborative seating, including the curved sofa and tub chairs of the executive lounge
  • Integrated storage and credenzas
  • Process: shop drawings, finish samples, hold-point inspections, installation

As photographed

Every photograph of the project, in the order of a walk through the space. Nothing is cropped.

Materials and making

As on every Calibro project, each piece was detailed in a shop drawing for BH Architects' approval, sampled in the specified finishes, produced in audited UAE partner workshops and checked against the approved drawing before installation in the office.
