Contract furniture and FF&E: one package, made and procured

Calibro is an FF&E supplier and procurement partner in Dubai: it supplies contract furniture and complete FF&E packages across the UAE: bespoke pieces made to the designer's drawings, specified items procured and checked, all delivered to one programme under one contract. Typical scopes are private residences and penthouses, building receptions, workplaces and hotel public areas.

What an FF&E package includes

Furniture, fixtures and equipment. OS&E only when the project agrees it.
Made to drawing
Sofas, seating, tables, beds, casegoods and feature pieces from the designer's drawings: bespoke furniture.
Fixed joinery
Wardrobes, panelling, vanities, media walls and reception desks where the package includes them: joinery.
Procured items
Specified furniture, lighting, rugs, mirrors and accessories, sourced as named and checked on arrival.
Delivery and installation
Consolidated, delivered, installed and handed over as one scope: installation and aftercare.
( 02 )

How procurement works

The specification is the reference. Nothing is swapped without approval.
  1. 01

    Specification review

    Every line of the schedule is checked for availability, lead time, budget and compliance before anything is ordered.

  2. 02

    Alternatives, approved

    Where an item is unavailable or outside budget, an alternative is proposed with samples or data sheets for the designer's written approval.

  3. 03

    Consolidation and inspection

    Procured items are received, inspected against the specification and held with the bespoke pieces until the site is ready.

  4. 04

    Delivery and installation

    One programme, one installation team, one snag list, one handover.

Questions about FF&E

What is the difference between FF&E and OS&E?

FF&E is furniture, fixtures and equipment: the loose furniture, fixed joinery, lighting, artwork and accessories that furnish a space. OS&E is operating supplies and equipment: the linen, tableware, housekeeping and back-of-house items a hotel runs on. Calibro supplies FF&E; OS&E is included only when a project agrees it.

Who holds the contract for an FF&E package?

Calibro. Bespoke pieces and procured items sit under one contract and one programme, so there is one point of responsibility for specification, delivery, installation and snagging.

Can you procure items from the designer's specification?

Yes. Specified items are sourced as named. Where a specified item is unavailable or outside budget, an alternative is proposed with samples or data sheets for the designer's written approval.

Do you supply hotel FF&E?

Yes, for public areas and receptions, with contract-grade construction, fire-rated foams and fabrics where the project specifies them, and mock-up pieces for approval before rollout. Calibro's hospitality references to date are receptions and lobbies; guestroom programmes are quoted project by project.
